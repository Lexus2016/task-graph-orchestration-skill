# Vendor contract policy (Legal, v3)

A vendor contract is compliant only if all three rules hold **after any signed amendments are applied**:

1. **Renewal** — if the contract renews automatically, the non-renewal notice period must be **30 days or less**. Contracts without automatic renewal pass this rule.
2. **Liability** — the liability cap must be **at least 12 months of fees**.
3. **Data residency** — Customer Data may be stored and processed **only in the European Union**.
